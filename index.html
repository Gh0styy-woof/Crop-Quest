Sunflower

Sunflowers are flowering plants that grow best in warm, sunny conditions.

Sunlight: Full sun

Water: Regular watering

Soil: Well-drained soil

Temperature: Warm conditions

Growing Time: About 70–100 days

Care Tip: Give sunflowers plenty of sunlight and enough space to grow.
